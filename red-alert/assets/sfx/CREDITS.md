# Sound effect credits

Recorded sound effects come from the **Sonniss GDC 2015 Game Audio Bundle** (https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc/): royalty-free for use in games, no attribution required. We credit the original creators anyway.
Files were trimmed, mixed to mono, loudness-normalized and converted to OGG for the game. Heartbeat, flatline, radio static and fallback tones are generated procedurally in code.
